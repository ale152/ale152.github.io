## Map your beta. Never forget a move.

Whether you climb sport, trad or boulder, you'll never forget a beta again.

Start with a photo of the wall. Add the holds. Set the moves in order. Broken Crimp
turns them into a **beta map** you can go over in your head, follow at the crag, or
send to a friend.

**[Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brokencrimp.app)**

## Draw the climb, not a description of it

Place holds on a photo of the real wall, from a catalogue of 37 types: crimps, jugs,
slopers, pockets and volumes, plus the bolts, cams and nuts a trad line runs on. Give
them nicknames. Add section dividers where the climb changes character.

## Record every move

Say which hand or foot goes where, and in what order. Mark two limbs moving at once.
Note a re-grip, a heel hook, a kneebar, a drop knee, a bat hang. Then play the
sequence back and watch it climb.

## A climber your own size

The figure on the wall is drawn to a height you set for each beta: tap the climber and
drag. It is not only cosmetic. A limb the sequence stretches beyond its own reach lets
go of the wall, so your height decides which hands and feet the app still shows on the
holds. Pin one down if you want it to stay.

## Remember the details that matter

Attach a close-up photo to a single hold, so you can see the exact edge you need.
Record a voice note while it's fresh. Write down what went wrong last time.

## Find it again

Search your climbs by name, by the name of a beta, by a note you wrote, or by what you
called a hold. Filter by boulder, sport or trad, and sort by when you last changed a
climb or when you added it.

## Share it

Send a climb to a friend as a file they can open in their own app. Export a PDF to
print out, a video of the sequence to send, or a plain list of the moves to paste into
a message. Back up everything to a single file you keep yourself.

## Works where you climb

No signal at the crag, no problem. Broken Crimp works entirely offline, and your
climbs, photos and voice notes stay on your phone. We run no servers and never
receive a copy of your data.

Available in English and Italian.

---

## Free, with ads

Broken Crimp is free, and ads are what pay for it. There is a banner under the climb
list, and one full-screen ad after you export a beta.

There are no ads while you are drawing holds, building a beta, or reading one at the
crag — and none when you open the app or open a climb.
