## 1. Safety warning — read this

> ### Climbing is dangerous.
>
> **Rock climbing, sport climbing, bouldering and all related activities carry an
> inherent and unavoidable risk of serious injury, permanent disability and death.
> That risk cannot be eliminated. By using this app you accept that risk yourself.**

**The app is a notebook, not instruction.** Broken Crimp lets you draw and store your
own diagrams of climbs. It does not teach climbing, it does not assess risk, and
nothing in it is professional instruction, coaching or safety advice. It is not a
substitute for training from a qualified instructor, for a competent climbing partner,
or for your own judgement.

**The drawn climber is an illustration, not a demonstration.** The app draws a figure
on the wall and plays your sequence back with it. That figure is generated from the
holds and limbs you recorded, at a size you choose. It is not a model of your body, it
is not a coach, and it does not show correct or safe technique. A position the figure
can be drawn in may be one you cannot reach or should not try.

**A beta map describes a sequence of moves and nothing else.** It says nothing about
whether a climb is safe for you, whether the holds are sound, whether the protection
or bolts are adequate or in good condition, whether the landing is safe, whether the
pads and spotters are sufficient, or whether the climb is within your ability.

**Content in the app may be wrong, out of date, or unsafe for you.**

* Holds break, spin and wear out. Rock breaks. Bolts and anchors age and fail.
* Gym routes are stripped and reset. A beta map of a gym problem may describe holds
  that are no longer on the wall.
* Conditions change with weather, temperature, seasoning and traffic.
* A sequence that works for one climber may be dangerous for another with different
  height, reach, strength, flexibility, experience or injury history.
* Beta shared with you by another person is **their** content. We do not review,
  verify, endorse or check any of it. We have no way to know whether it is accurate
  or safe.

**You are responsible for your own safety.** Before and while climbing, you alone are
responsible for assessing the climb and the conditions, inspecting your equipment,
checking your knots, harness, belay and anchors, judging your own ability and fitness,
choosing competent partners, and deciding whether to climb at all. Always follow
recognised safety practice and the rules of the venue you are at.

**Do not use the app while climbing in a way that compromises your safety** — do not
read your phone while belaying, while on lead, or anywhere that a lapse in attention
could hurt you or someone else.
